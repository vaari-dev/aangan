# Google Play store listing

Copy each field into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

## App name (30 characters max)

    Aangan: Private TV Home Screen

## Short description (80 characters max)

    A calm, ad-free home screen for your TV: apps, weather, air quality, festivals.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Aangan is a calm, private home screen for Android TV and Google TV. It replaces the stock home screen, with its rows of ads and recommendations, with your apps, the sky outside and the days your family celebrates.

YOUR APPS, AND NOTHING ELSE
• A row of favourites across the top and every other app in a tidy grid.
• Hold OK on any app to move it, pin it or hide it.
• No sponsored rows, no autoplaying trailers, nothing promoted.

THE SKY OUTSIDE
• The time, weather and air quality for your city, always in the corner.
• Press Up then OK for the whole day: the next hours, high and low, sunrise and sunset, UV and the moon.
• A 36-hour PM2.5 trend against the safe limit, on India's CPCB scale in India and the US EPA scale elsewhere, and the best time to air out the house.

FESTIVALS AND FAMILY DAYS
• A countdown to the next festival, and on the day a hand-drawn scene behind your apps: diyas for Diwali, gulal for Holi, the moon through the chalni for Karwa Chauth, lanterns for Eid, lights and snow for Christmas, and more.
• Add birthdays and anniversaries; Aangan celebrates them with balloons or petals and a greeting by name.
• Your family keeps a festival on another day? Move it, or hide one you don't celebrate.

SET UP FROM YOUR PHONE
• Scan the code in Settings to choose your city and add dates from your phone's browser. The TV serves the page itself, on your Wi-Fi, only while Settings is open.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, analytics, crash reporting or account.
• No location permission: you choose your city.
• Your family's dates stay on the TV and are excluded from cloud backup.
• The only outside service is Open-Meteo, for your city's forecast.

LIGHT ON YOUR TV
• About 150 KB, with no third-party libraries.
• Nothing moves on an ordinary day, so it uses no processor time while you watch.

OPEN SOURCE
• Every line is public under the MIT licence: github.com/vaari-dev/aangan

OPEN WITH THE HOME BUTTON
Most TVs offer no setting to choose a home screen. Aangan can optionally use an accessibility service to open itself when you press Home or when the TV's own home screen appears. It reacts to the Home button only, every other button passes straight through, and it can't see what's on screen. Aangan shows this explanation and asks for your agreement before you switch it on.

Weather and air quality by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) and Copernicus CAMS. Festival dates follow Drik Panchang for New Delhi.
```
