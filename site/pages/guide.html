The home screen

  • Time and date, with the next festival or family day within a month: "Diwali in 14 days", "Holi tomorrow", or the greeting on the day.
  • The weather pill: temperature, conditions, feels-like and air quality, coloured by how healthy the air is.
  • The dock: four apps you choose, larger than the rest.
  • Every other app, then Aangan's Settings and, until Aangan opens with the Home button, Set up Aangan.

Tips run along the bottom the first eight times the home screen opens.

App info

The dock stays as you leave it. It starts with your old top row, if you had one, or installed streaming apps such as Netflix, YouTube and Prime Video, then the rest alphabetically. Choosing an app already in the dock swaps the two. Aangan never reorders it; an uninstalled app's place shows a + until you fill it. You can also set it in Settings or from your phone.

To bring back hidden apps, hold OK on Settings and choose Show hidden apps. New apps appear in the grid automatically.

Weather and air quality

Weather is for one city, Gurgaon until you choose. Aangan has no location permission.

In Settings → City, pick a large Indian city, or type a few letters: matches appear with their state and size, to tell same-named places apart. Old names work: Bombay, Bangalore, Calcutta, Madras, Gurgaon.

  • Weather refreshes at most every 30 minutes, air quality every hour, only while the home screen is showing.
  • In India, air quality uses the CPCB National AQI (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe), from PM2.5 and PM10; elsewhere, the US EPA scale.
  • Offline, the last reading stays. After three hours the pill fades and the screensaver drops the weather.

The Today card

▲ to the weather pill, then OK. Back closes it.

  • Weather: the next six hours, the high and low, and a note if rain is likely soon.
  • The day: sunrise, sunset, hours of daylight, the UV peak and the moon's phase.
  • Air: the 24-hour average, PM2.5 and PM10, what it means for health, and a 36-hour PM2.5 chart against the country's safe limit.
  • Best time to air out the house: the cleanest stretch in the next 18 hours, if any.
  • Coming up: the next four festivals and family days.

Festivals

The main Indian festivals and national days, from Lohri to Christmas. See the calendar.

  • Before: a countdown beside the date, from 30 days ahead.
  • On the day: a scene opens behind your apps and keeps moving gently while the home screen is showing. The screensaver shows the greeting.
  • Festival weeks: days between two entries with the same scene, up to nine days apart, share it, so all of Diwali week and Navratri glow.
  • Moon dates: Eid depends on a sighting, so its date is "expected" and may move by a day.

Moving or hiding a festival

In Settings → Festivals, press OK on one:

  • Change the date, within a year of the calendar's.
  • Hide it for the year, or Show it again.
  • Back to the calendar's date.

Birthdays and anniversaries

Add them in Settings or from your phone: a name as you'd say it ("Asha", "Dadi", "Asha & Rohan"), birthday or anniversary, and the day. No year is stored.

  • They join the countdown and the Today card.
  • On the day: balloons and confetti for a birthday, gold rings, hearts and rose petals for an anniversary, and "Happy birthday, Asha" by the clock.
  • A birthday on 29 February is celebrated on the 28th in other years.
  • A family day beats a festival on the same day.

From your phone

While Settings is open, scan its QR code with your phone on the same Wi-Fi to do everything Settings does, with a real keyboard.

  • The TV serves the page, only while Settings is open, at an address with a fresh secret, on your home network only.
  • Browsers may call it "not secure" because it's plain HTTP; see the FAQ.
  • Changes appear on the TV as you make them.

The screensaver

A dim clock with the date, weather and any greeting, drifting so nothing burns in. Choose Aangan clock in Settings → Screensaver or the TV's screensaver settings. Recent Google TVs allow only Google's Ambient mode.

The Home button

With Open with Home on, Home and switching the TV on bring up Aangan in place of the TV's own home screen. To stop, switch Aangan off in the TV's accessibility settings, or uninstall it.
