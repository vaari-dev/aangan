Questions

Frequently asked.

Can't find your answer? Ask on GitHub.

What is Aangan?

A home screen (launcher) for Android TV and Google TV. It replaces the stock screen's ads and recommendations with your apps, the weather and air quality, and countdowns to festivals and family birthdays. Free, open source, and it sends nothing to its makers.

Does it remove the ads from my TV's home screen?

It replaces the screen they're on. Apps such as streaming services still show what they show inside them.

Which TVs does it work on?

Any TV or box with Android TV or Google TV 9 (Pie) or later: Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, Xiaomi Mi TV and Mi Box, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield and others. It's developed on Sony Bravia (Android TV 9, Google TV 12) and each release is checked on emulators for Android TV 13 and Google TV 16. Not Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku or Fire TV; here's why.

Why is there no Screensaver option on my TV?

Recent Google TVs allow only Google's Ambient mode, so Aangan hides the option there. Elsewhere, choose Aangan clock.

Why doesn't it work on Samsung TVs?

Samsung TVs run Tizen, not Android, and it doesn't allow this:

  • No Android apps. Tizen apps are web or .NET apps written for Tizen.
  • The home screen is Samsung's. Smart Hub is part of the system, with no way to choose another.
  • So is the Home button. Apps can claim some keys, but never Home.
  • No easy installs. Outside apps need developer mode, Samsung's tools and a certificate, and still can't replace built-in ones.

LG's webOS and Roku are closed the same way. Fire TV is Android, but Amazon has blocked other home screens since 2022.

Instead: plug in a Google TV or Android TV streamer (Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV, Xiaomi TV Box) and install Aangan there. Turn on Anynet+ (HDMI-CEC) so one remote controls both. More on the install page.

Do I need developer mode or a computer?

No. Install with the free Downloader app, then use the welcome's Open with Home. See how.

Why does it need an accessibility service?

Most TVs can't choose a different home screen, and without a computer this is the only way for an app to answer the Home button. The service watches only the Home key and the TV's own home screen appearing; other buttons pass through, and it can't read the screen. It's optional; see the privacy policy.

Will it slow my TV down?

No. It's about 150 KB with no libraries, and on an ordinary day nothing moves, so it uses no processor time. Weather is fetched only while the home screen is showing, at most every 30 minutes.

My phone says the setup page is "not secure". Is it safe?

Yes, on your home Wi-Fi. Browsers warn about any page without HTTPS, and a TV can't get a certificate without a cloud service, which Aangan avoids. Choose to continue. What you type never leaves your network, and the page stops when you leave Settings.

The QR code doesn't open on my phone.

Put the phone on the TV's Wi-Fi, not mobile data or a guest network, and keep Settings open on the TV. Some routers isolate devices; then use the TV instead.

Why doesn't it use my location?

It doesn't need it. You choose your city, and the forecast sends its coordinates rounded to about a kilometre.

Where are my family's birthdays stored?

Only on the TV, in Aangan's private storage, excluded from cloud backup. Uninstalling deletes them. To move them, export a backup from the phone page.

Our family celebrates a festival on a different day.

Move it, or hide it for the year, in Settings or on the phone page. See how.

Can I add my own festival or a regional one?

Not in the app. Suggest it on GitHub, or add the one line to the calendar yourself. Meanwhile, add it as an anniversary.

How do I hide an app or change the dock?

Hold OK on the app and choose Put in the dock or Hide, or use Settings or your phone. Hidden apps come back from the Settings tile's menu. More.

Does Aangan send crash reports?

No. A crash leaves a short note on the TV (app and Android versions, TV model, where in the code it failed), and Settings says so. The phone page shows it and can open a pre-filled GitHub issue for you to send yourself, or delete it.

How do I go back to the TV's own home screen?

Uninstall Aangan from Settings → Apps, or switch off its service in accessibility settings. Aangan changes nothing on your TV.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet; until then, use Downloader. Both are signed with the same key, so you can switch without losing anything.

Who makes it, and how is it free?

Vaari, as open source, first for a family's own TVs. With no servers it costs nothing to run, and there's nothing to sell, including your data.

Can I trust a download from GitHub?

Every release is built from the public source and signed with one key, whose fingerprint is on the install page. Android refuses updates signed with any other key.

How can I help?

Report problems or suggest festivals on GitHub, try a TV we haven't, or send a pull request; start with the developer guide.
